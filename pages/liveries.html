Private server liveries

Private servers can use custom liveries. Submissions are checked automatically, and anything the check can't be sure about goes to manual approval.

  1. Create your livery
    Design it in the in-game livery maker.
  2. Send it in
    Submit it for approval from your private server.
  3. Get approved
    Once approved, your livery is ready to use on your server.
